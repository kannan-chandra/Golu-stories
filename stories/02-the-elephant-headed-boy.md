Parvati (பார்வதி) created a boy named Ganesha (கணேசர் / விநாயகர்) and asked him to guard her doorway. When Shiva (சிவன்) arrived, the boy refused to let him enter. A battle followed, and Shiva cut off his head. Seeing Parvati’s grief, Shiva restored the boy to life with an elephant’s head. Ganesha became a beloved god whom people pray to before beginning something new.
